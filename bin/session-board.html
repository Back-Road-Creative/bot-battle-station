DEFCON: the box's level, worst first

LevelWhenWhat you do
DEFCON 1A session NEEDS YOU.Open it (1-9 or click) and answer. The only level with a siren.
DEFCON 2Something the board measured looks off: a job behind schedule, a helper silent, a session near full context, or the board itself offline (it could hide a new ask).Glance at the amber tile. Usually nothing; act only if it stays amber.
DEFCON 3Sessions wait on a job (CI, a test run).Nothing. They resume on their own.
DEFCON 4Sessions are working.Nothing.
DEFCON 5All quiet: every session is idle.Give one the next task.

Session states

StateHow it is decidedWhat you do
NEEDS YOUClaude Code reports a prompt open (permission, question, plan), or the last reply holds a command for you to run.Answer the prompt, or run the command.
RUNNINGClaude Code reports the session busy.Nothing.
WAITING ON JOBA background job it started has not finished, and its process is still alive.Nothing; it wakes when the job ends.
IDLEAnything else: the turn is over. "for you:" shows its suggested next step and "waits on:" another session it named, never an alarm.Read it when convenient.

Amber warnings (measured, never from a reply's wording)

WarningHow it is measuredWhat you do
job behind scheduleRunning past the 80th percentile of at least 4 finished runs of the same kind in that session. The tile shows "usually X, now Y".If a test run: a hang or a slow box. Look at the session's last output; stop it if it hangs.
helper silent 20m+A helper agent's log has not changed for 20 minutes.Open the session; the helper may be stuck on a long command.
board offlineA full-width amber banner after 15 s of failed polls in a row (each times out at 5 s); before that the badge only reads RETRYING. The tiles dim because they are stale. "board page error" is the same banner when the board answers but the page cannot draw it.Offline: restart the board. Page error: reload; if it stays, it is a page bug.
context near full90% of the session's own auto-compact size (else this box's usual).Let it finish its step, then start it fresh or let it compact.
